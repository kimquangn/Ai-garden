How it connects · high level

One battery, one panel, one Wi-Fi link

The physical build is in the build guide. This page is the simple picture of how the pieces talk.

  1. 20 W solar panel on the shed roof
  2. ↓ charge controller
  3. Battery 12.8 V 6 Ah in the shed, fused
  4. ↓ 2-core cable to the bed
  5. ESP32 node at the bed: valve, soil probes, flow meter
  6. ↓ 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi
  7. Home router → AI app + dashboard on a home computer or phone

One battery pack

A small LiFePO4 battery in the shed runs everything at the bed: the ESP32, the 12 V valve and the sensors. Buy it new, with a fuse at the battery and on the panel.

The solar

A 20 W 12 V panel on the shed roof or fence top, facing north, through a charge controller with a LiFePO4 setting. A motorised roof later would need a bigger panel and battery.

The Wi-Fi link

The ESP32 joins home Wi-Fi and reports to the AI app on your network; no cloud hub needed. With 2 bars at the bed, start with an ESP32 that takes an external 5 dBi antenna (~$12–30). Add a mesh node or repeater only if readings drop out.

If the app or Wi-Fi goes down

The node keeps itself safe: the valve closes after a maximum run time, the flow meter flags leaks, and charging stops if the battery is below 2 °C or above 45 °C.
