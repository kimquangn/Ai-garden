一本仕立て · Training & pruning

One stem, on a string, in the light

Manual now · camera prompt: later

Tomatoes are trained to one stem (一本仕立て) and tied to a string (誘引). Side shoots are pinched out so light and air reach every truss.

The Japanese evidence

  • Single-stem training (一本仕立て) with weekly side-shoot removal (芽かき) and string support (誘引) is the default in Japanese protected tomato production guides. NARO vegetable manuals
  • Benefits: better airflow, less disease and more even trusses. For this bed it means 4 cherry tomatoes on strings from the fence trellis.

How the AI system handles it

  1. Now

    Cherry tomatoes on 1–2 leaders on strings from a stainless trellis wire; side shoots removed weekly; plants topped by late February so the last trusses ripen.

  2. Later (camera)

    The daily photo gives a rough timing prompt: "growth is fast this week; good time to pinch side shoots and lower leaves". It does not mark every shoot.

  3. Now

    A weekly reminder in the app, timed to growth from the heat-unit (GDD) count.

What you need

Stainless wire, eye bolts, twine. About $40–75 (in Stage 1).

Honest limits

The reminder is a nudge; the plant still needs your eyes.
