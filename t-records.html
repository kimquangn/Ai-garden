糖度 · Measure & record

Measure sugar, keep records, learn each season

Stage 1

Hardware: Stage 1 being built · App: in development, nothing is live yet · Controller: Kim's custom local app talking to the ESP32

Japanese fruit is graded by sugar (Brix), size and skin. Measuring is what turns these methods into results you can check.

The Japanese evidence

  • A handheld optical refractometer (0–32 %Brix) is the standard tool for fruit quality.
  • Myth: "high Brix makes plants pest-proof". Not supported by evidence. WSU CSANR

How the AI system will handle it

  1. Measure

    After picking: Brix from a drop of juice, plus weight on a kitchen scale.

  2. Log

    You will enter it in Kim's custom local app in a few taps. It will be stored with that plant's watering, thinning and weather history.

  3. Analyse

    At season end, the app will compare which watering level and thinning gave the sweetest fruit for the yield, and propose next season's settings.

What you need

Refractometer ~$20–40, kitchen scale.

Honest limits

One small bed gives small samples. Expect trends, not proof.
