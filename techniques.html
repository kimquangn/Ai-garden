Japanese methods

Six techniques, and how the AI runs each

Each page covers what the technique is, the Japanese evidence, and exactly what the sensors and app do. All are applied from research and commercial practice; this bed is the home test.

01 · フルーツトマト

Controlled water stress

Japanese "fruit tomatoes" (フルーツトマト) are ordinary tomatoes grown with deliberately less water once the fruit starts to swell.

02 · 雨よけ栽培

Rain shelter

Rain shelters (雨よけ) are clear roofs over the crop with open sides.

03 · 摘果

Fewer fruit per plant

Japanese melon growers keep one fruit per vine; strawberry and tomato growers remove the smallest fruit and flowers.

04 · 一本仕立て

Training & pruning

Tomatoes are trained to one stem (一本仕立て) and tied to a string (誘引).

05 · 土づくり

Living soil

土づくり, soil building, is the base of Japanese growing: compost, mulch and a green-manure crop over winter, so the soil holds water and feeds slowly..

Measure & record

Skipped on purpose

  • Biochar / rice-husk charcoal for yield: no temperate yield benefit
  • Salt stress in a mixed bed: harms berries and greens
  • EM ferment sprays as pest cures
  • "High Brix = pest-proof": a myth
