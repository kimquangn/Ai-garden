フルーツトマト · Controlled water stress

Sweeter tomatoes from controlled water stress

Stage 1

Hardware: Stage 1 being built · App: in development, nothing is live yet · Controller: Kim's custom local app talking to the ESP32

Japanese "fruit tomatoes" (フルーツトマト) are ordinary tomatoes grown with deliberately less water once the fruit starts to swell. The fruit stays smaller but the sugar is more concentrated, so they taste much sweeter.

The Japanese evidence

  • Greenhouse trials: mild drought raised sugar concentration; small deficits improved quality without hurting yield. Much of the gain is less water in the fruit, not more sugar made. Acta Hort. 1170
  • A Wakayama prefectural trial used soil-moisture sensors to hold soil at pF 2.7–2.9 and steadily raised Brix. NARO/Wakayama
  • Timing matters: stress from early fruit swell gave Brix 9.2, from peak swell 8.0, from mature green only 6.6.
  • Too much stress causes blossom-end rot and tiny fruit. NARO

How the AI system will handle it

  1. Sense (planned)

    A soil-moisture probe will sit in each of the 2 deficit fabric grow bags (40–50 L) set into the bed, with a third probe in the bed itself. Bags restrict the roots so the stress can actually build; a deep 0.6 m bed would buffer it.

  2. Decide

    From first truss colour, Kim's custom local app will lower the watering threshold for those bags one step at a time, at most one step a week. A third bag will be watered normally as the comparison.

  3. Act

    The app will tell the ESP32 to open valve 2 (zone 2: the two deficit bags) only, using the average of the two bag probes. Zone 1 (the bed and the control bag) will be watered normally by valve 1 from the bed probe.

  4. Check

    You will log Brix and any blossom-end rot each week; the app will back off if rot appears and hold the level that gives the best Brix.

  5. Planned starting points

    Zone 2 deficit begins at first truss colour. Bag probes are let fall to about 25–30% VWC before watering, against about 35–45% for zone 1. Target Brix 8–10 for cherry tomatoes. Maximum one step change per week. Refractometer readings logged weekly. Starting points, to be calibrated on site.

What you need

2 deficit bags + 1 control bag, a second drip zone with its own 12 V valve, 3 soil probes, refractometer (can add later). All in the Stage 1 list.

Honest limits

Expect sweeter fruit and roughly 20–30% less weight from the stressed plants. No salt: it builds up and harms berries and greens.
