フルーツトマト · Controlled water stress

Sweeter tomatoes from controlled water stress

Stage 1

Japanese "fruit tomatoes" (フルーツトマト) are ordinary tomatoes grown with deliberately less water once the fruit starts to swell. The fruit stays smaller but the sugar is more concentrated, so they taste much sweeter.

The Japanese evidence

  • Greenhouse trials: mild drought raised sugar concentration; small deficits improved quality without hurting yield. Much of the gain is less water in the fruit, not more sugar made. Acta Hort. 1170
  • A Wakayama prefectural trial used soil-moisture sensors to hold soil at pF 2.7–2.9 and steadily raised Brix. NARO/Wakayama
  • Timing matters: stress from early fruit swell gave Brix 9.2, from peak swell 8.0, from mature green only 6.6.
  • Too much stress causes blossom-end rot and tiny fruit. NARO

How the AI system handles it

  1. Sense

    A soil-moisture probe sits in each of 2 fabric grow bags (40–50 L) set into the bed. Bags restrict the roots so the stress can actually build; a deep 0.6 m bed would buffer it.

  2. Decide

    About 3 weeks after first fruit set, the app lowers the watering threshold for those bags a step at a time. A third bag is watered normally as the comparison.

  3. Act

    The ESP32 opens a 12 V valve on that drip zone only. The rest of the bed is unaffected.

  4. Check

    You log Brix and any blossom-end rot each week; the app backs off if rot appears and holds the level that gives the best Brix.

What you need

2 + 1 fabric bags, a separate drip zone, 12 V valve, soil probes, refractometer. Covered in the Stage 1 list.

Honest limits

Expect sweeter fruit and roughly 20–30% less weight from the stressed plants. No salt: it builds up and harms berries and greens.
