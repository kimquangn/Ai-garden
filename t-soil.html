土づくり · Living soil

Build the soil, then measure it

Moisture: Stage 1 · EC/temp probe: Stage 2

土づくり, soil building, is the base of Japanese growing: compost, mulch and a green-manure crop over winter, so the soil holds water and feeds slowly.

The Japanese evidence

  • Steady organic matter, mulch and a winter cover crop (broad beans, oats, field peas) improve structure and nitrogen. Standard extension advice, done consistently.
  • Charcoal and biochar are skipped: no yield benefit shown in temperate soils.

How the AI system handles it

  1. Now (Stage 1)

    Capacitive soil-moisture probes show how fast the bed dries. As organic matter builds, the curve flattens.

  2. Stage 2

    A combined soil probe (moisture, temperature, EC) shows when nutrients run low and when the soil is warm enough to plant.

  3. By hand

    A $15 pH kit each season and a lab soil test once a year. You type the results in; the app trends them.

  4. Suggest

    The app suggests when to add compost and when to sow or chop green manure, based on the season and the trends.

What you need

Compost, mulch, green-manure seed (~$10). Probes in Stage 1–2.

Honest limits

Cheap probes drift; treat EC as a trend, not a lab number.
