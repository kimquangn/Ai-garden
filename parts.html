Parts & costs · AUD · checked Nov 2026

Stage 1: $1,347 lean, $1,382 full

Two figures, used on every page: lean leaves out the refractometer and pH kit (marked can add later); full includes them. Stage 1a (bed + smart watering) is $987 lean / $1,022 full, near the ~$1,000 target; the fixed roof (1b) adds $360, so all of Stage 1 is over that target. Stage 1a is ~$987 lean rather than the original ~$927 list because it adds the second valve zone, 3 probes instead of 2, the trellis hardware and a tenth post.

Second-hand first: prices are what similar items were listed for on Facebook Marketplace around Melbourne, or the cheapest new price where buying used is unsafe (battery, fuses, backflow valve, stainless fixings). Buying everything new costs roughly 2–3× more.
